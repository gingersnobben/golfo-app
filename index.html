The course map doesn't show greens or holes

Golfo uses OpenStreetMap, which is mapped by volunteers. Some courses are missing or only partly mapped. You can always place the green yourself with Set green, and Golfo remembers it for that course.

The distance seems wrong

Distances depend on your iPhone's GPS. Make sure Golfo is allowed to use your precise location (Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Golfo). Open sky works best.

Where is my data?

Your rounds are stored only on your iPhone. You can export them as a CSV file from the app.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Golfo is built to keep your golf data on your iPhone. There is no account, no advertising and no tracking.

Data stored on your device

Your rounds, scores, statistics, clubs and course notes (such as greens you have placed) are stored locally on your iPhone. They are not sent to us. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Location

Golfo uses your location while you use the app to show distances on the course map. To do this, information about the area around your position is sent to these services:

  • Golfo's website (GitHub Pages) – course map files for your area are downloaded from this site, which is hosted by GitHub.
  • OpenStreetMap data servers (Overpass) – used if the course is not in Golfo's files.
  • Open-Meteo – weather, wind and elevation for the area.

These services receive the requested area and, like any website, your IP address. Golfo does not send your name or any account details. Golfo does not store your location history on a server.

Apple Intelligence

Round analysis and tips are created on your device with Apple's on-device models, where available.

Analytics and crash reports

Golfo has no analytics of its own. If you have chosen to share data with app developers in iOS, Apple may provide anonymous crash reports.

Children

Golfo does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone.
